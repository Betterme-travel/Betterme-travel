Good To Know

Questions travelers ask us

A few things people want to know before they write to us. If your question is not here, send it with your inquiry — a designer will answer personally.

How is a tailor-made journey actually built?

It begins with a conversation. We listen to your dates, pace, interests and any health goals, then a designer writes a single itinerary just for you — hotels, guides, treatments and free time chosen around the way you actually like to travel. Nothing is a fixed package; every day can be adjusted before you confirm.

How large are your groups?

Most Better Me Travel journeys are private, so it is just you and your party. The only shared departures we run are our school and senior group trips, and we keep those deliberately small so guides can give everyone real attention.

Can I travel on my own?

Absolutely — many of our travelers come solo and tell us it is the most restful way to go. We choose accommodation and guides that feel safe and welcoming for single travelers, and we flag clearly in advance if any hotel charges a single-room supplement, so there are no surprises.

What about dietary needs and allergies?

Tell us in your inquiry and we pass it to every chef, hotel and retreat along the way. Menus are built with local chefs around seasonal food, so vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and allergy-aware meals are planned in advance rather than sorted out on the day.

How far ahead should I book?

For most journeys three to four months gives us a comfortable choice of hotels and the best guides. Peak seasons and school group trips are worth planning six to nine months ahead — but if your dates are sooner, write to us anyway, as we can often still make space.
